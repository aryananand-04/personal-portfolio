02 — North Star Metric

The metric that
actually matters

Zepto's stated NSM is orders per day. That's wrong for the IPO stage they're entering.

North Star Metric
Contribution margin per order, improving month-on-month
Zepto processed millions of orders in FY25 and posted ₹3,367 Cr in losses. More orders at negative contribution margin is not growth — it's accelerated bleeding. Every product decision should be evaluated against whether it moves contribution margin per order toward positive.

05 — Gap Analysis

Product gaps
RICE scored

Three gaps between Zepto's current product and a business that can go public with improving unit economics.

01
Critical
Free delivery below breakeven basket size is destroying unit economics
Zepto's net losses grew 177% in FY25 — faster than revenue growth of 129%. A significant driver is delivery cost on small-basket orders. Zepto's checkout UI does not dynamically surface the basket-size needed for free delivery, nor does it recommend add-ons calibrated to bridge that gap. Every small-basket free-delivery order is a loss.

06 — Recommendations

Now / Next / Later
roadmap

Three initiatives sequenced for the pre-IPO window Zepto is currently in.

Now · 0–3 months
Dynamic basket builder at checkout
Show the exact amount needed to hit free-delivery threshold. Surface 2–3 AI-recommended add-ons based on purchase history. Drives basket size up, delivery cost per order down, gives users perceived value.
KR1 — Average order value: +15% within 60 days
KR2 — % orders above free-delivery threshold: +20pp
KR3 — Contribution margin per order: trending positive in pilot cities
Next · 3–9 months
Zepto Pass — aggressive push at every high-intent touchpoint
Surface Zepto Pass at checkout, post-delivery, and in onboarding. Offer a 30-day free trial. Bundle Pass with Zepto Café where available. A subscriber ordering 12× per month generates more revenue and costs less per order.
KR1 — Zepto Pass subscriber base: +3x in 6 months
KR2 — Subscriber order frequency vs non-subscriber: measure and report
KR3 — Pass revenue as % of total revenue: visible and growing
Later · 9–18 months
Retail media platform — brand advertising dashboard
Build a self-serve ad platform for FMCG brands to buy sponsored placements. Price on CPC or CPM. The 25–34 urban demographic is exactly what HUL, P&G, Marico, and D2C brands want to reach. Retail media gross margins are 70%+.
KR1 — Retail media revenue: ₹500 Cr ARR within 12 months
KR2 — Brand advertisers on platform: 200+ in first 6 months
KR3 — Retail media as % of revenue: visible line on IPO prospectus
07 — PM View

The bottom line

"Zepto grew 129% in FY25. Its losses grew 177%. The product is winning users. The business model isn't winning yet."

The IPO DRHP was filed in December 2025. Public market investors will read these numbers differently from private investors. ₹3,367 Cr in losses on ₹9,669 Cr in revenue — with losses growing faster than revenue — is a narrative Zepto needs to change before listing.
The product foundation is strong: 70.58M downloads, 45% ARPU growth, 10-minute delivery at scale. The gap is monetisation. Dynamic basket building, Zepto Pass penetration, and retail media are all margin levers inside the product team's control.
Blinkit is at 46% market share and turned EBITDA positive. Zepto at 25% is not a distant second — it's the last credible challenger. But credibility at IPO requires a clearer path to profitability.
