02 — North Star Metric

The metric that
actually matters

Post-Notion 3.0, the old NSM (pages created per user) is irrelevant. The platform has shifted.

North Star Metric
Weekly tasks completed by AI Agents per workspace
Notion's value proposition shifted from "organised notes" to "AI that does work for you." The only metric that validates this shift is autonomous task completion. Workspaces where Agents complete recurring tasks weekly are the ones that stay and expand. Every product decision post-3.0 should move this number.

06 — Recommendations

Now / Next / Later
roadmap

Three initiatives sequenced to move from "AI add-on" to "AI-native workspace."

Now · 0–3 months
Pre-built Agent library — 50 ready-to-run templates
Ship 50 pre-built Agent templates for common use cases: standup summariser, project status updater, meeting notes → action items. One-click install, zero configuration. Surface in onboarding and on every database page.
KR1 — Agent activation rate: → 25% of AI subscribers in 60 days
KR2 — Time-to-first-agent-run: <10 minutes for 80% of new AI users
KR3 — Weekly active agent workspaces: +3x in 90 days
Next · 3–9 months
Bundle AI into Business plan — remove the add-on gate
Include Notion AI with a monthly usage quota in the Business plan at no extra cost. Charge overages above the quota. AI bundled = AI adopted at scale.
KR1 — Business plan AI adoption: → 70% of seats in 6 months
KR2 — Net revenue retention: maintain 120% via quota overages
KR3 — Enterprise upsell from Business: +25% YoY
Later · 9–18 months
Workspace-aware AI memory using existing vector infrastructure
Build persistent AI memory across a workspace using Notion's existing vector search infrastructure (already scaled 10x, costs down 90%). Notion AI should know your team structure, recurring projects, and past decisions.
KR1 — AI response relevance: +40% improvement
KR2 — Enterprise workspaces with memory: 60% in 12 months
KR3 — Enterprise 12-month retention: above 85%
07 — PM View

The bottom line

"Notion built a $500M business by removing friction. Charging $10/user extra for AI is the most un-Notion decision they've made."

Notion 3.0 was the right move. Agents at the centre is the correct long-term bet. But betting on Agents while keeping them behind a paywall and requiring technical setup is a contradiction.
50%+ of ARR from AI-enabled customers confirms the demand. The infrastructure is there. The gap is product distribution, not capability. Pre-built Agent templates would unlock a feature that exists but is inaccessible.
Microsoft is the real threat. M365 Copilot is embedded in tools enterprises already pay for, with no extra line item. Notion's answer must be speed of value delivery. An Agent running in under 10 minutes beats Copilot's capability every time.
